Privacy Policy

SplitEquity by Gift Bytes Productions · Last updated: 9 August 2026

This policy describes how SplitEquity (“the App”) handles information when you use our household expense and asset splitting service.

Information we collect

  • Account data: email address and display name you provide at sign-up.
  • Profile data: optional profile photo you upload.
  • Household data: household name, invite codes, income-split percentages, expenses, assets, and optional receipt images.
  • Purchase data: subscription status processed by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat (we do not store full payment card numbers).

How we use information

  • To operate household ledgers, settle-up, and exit-strategy calculations.
  • To authenticate you and sync data across devices.
  • To provide and verify Household Premium subscriptions.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.

Processors

We use Supabase (auth, database, file storage), Expo/EAS (app distribution), and RevenueCat plus Apple/Google for in-app purchases. Their processing is governed by their own policies.

Sharing

Household members you invite can see shared household data (transactions, members, premium status). We do not sell your personal data.

Retention

We keep account and household data while your account is active. You may request deletion by emailing support. Backups may persist for a limited period.

Your choices

  • Update your profile in the App.
  • Leave a household or delete uploaded photos/receipts where the App allows.
  • Contact us to request account deletion.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data.
